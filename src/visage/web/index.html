A CLEARER VIEW OF VISAGE

Built to compare. Easy to explore.

An application for understanding three approaches to age-group classification.

Appearance offers an estimate.

VisAge classifies a selected face into Under 18, 18–59, or 60+. It helps demonstrate how computer vision can support staff ID-check guidance. A person’s appearance does not establish their actual age or eligibility.

MODERN CONVOLUTION

ConvNeXt V2 Nano

A published ConvNeXt V2 Nano backbone with FCMAE pretraining and ImageNet adaptation. A replacement classifier estimates three age groups.

Pretrained backbone · Three-class softmax head
EFFICIENT CONVOLUTION

EfficientNetV2-S

Published EfficientNetV2-S weights adapted with a three-class head, evaluated using the same data partitions and training budget.

Pretrained backbone · Three-class softmax head
HIERARCHICAL TRANSFORMER

Swin Transformer V2 Tiny

Published Swin Transformer V2 Tiny weights using shifted-window attention, adapted for the same three age groups.

Pretrained backbone · Three-class softmax head

How a photo becomes a result

    Upload a still image or capture one with your camera.

    Explore the differences, then verify age with valid identification.

Privacy is part of the workflow.

Photos are processed in memory for the request. They are not saved, added to training, or included in the evaluation dashboard.

Camera access starts only when requested. Leaving camera mode or this page stops its video tracks.

No identity recognition, liveness verification or automatic purchase/discount approval is performed.
